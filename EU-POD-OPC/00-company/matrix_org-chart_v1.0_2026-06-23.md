## RACI — quy trình cross-department chính

| Activity | Owner | Product | Merch | Growth | Fulfill | Backoffice |
|----------|-------|---------|-------|--------|---------|-----------|
| Chọn niche/đợt promote | A | R | C | C | I | I |
| AOP design + IP clearance | I | R | I | I | — | C |
| GPSR clearance (EU) | A | I | C | I | — | R |
| Publish product + page | I | C | R | I | — | C(GPSR) |
| Chạy FB Ads | A | I | I | R | I | C(budget) |
| Route đơn ≤24h | I | — | — | I | R | I |
| Profit-per-SKU / P&L | A | I | I | C | C | R |

R = Responsible · A = Accountable · C = Consulted · I = Informed

## Gate cứng (hard gates)
- **No GPSR clearance → no publish** (Backoffice → Merchandising)
- **No Meta Ad Policy pass → no ads** (Backoffice/Compliance → Growth)
- **No IP/TM clearance → no listing** (Product Studio nội bộ)
