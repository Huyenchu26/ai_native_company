| 9 | **Cart-recovery rate** | đơn thu hồi / cart abandon | **≥ 10%** | weekly | GRW-003 |
| 10 | **Revenue-per-email** | revenue / email gửi | ≥ benchmark | per campaign | GRW-003 |
